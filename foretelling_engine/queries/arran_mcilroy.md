| Mac | *Mac* | kinship prefix | "son of" | Standard Old Irish grammar |

| Ruaidh | *ruadh* (genitive *ruaidh*) | adjective | "red, red-haired" | eDIL; Ó Corráin & Maguire 1981 |

### Combined Meaning
- **Literal:** "Árann, son of the servant of the red one."
- **Rendered:** *"He of the Threshold Islands, Son of the Devoted Servant of the Red-Haired Lord."*

### Position in Tradition's Naming Grammar
The surname is a canonical Gaelic patronymic constructed in the *Mac [X] + Giolla + [epithet]* pattern. This is the same kinship grammar the *Lebor Gabála Érenn* uses to trace Milesian descent (Míl → Breogán → Goidel Glas → Nél → Fénius Farsaid). The given name *Árann* ties the bearer to a specific landscape that is directly inside his own ancestral homeland — the Aran Islands off the coast of Galway.
