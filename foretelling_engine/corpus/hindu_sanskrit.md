## Pattern Axes

### Names
Sanskrit naming is extensively theophoric and descriptive:
- Names compound with *-deva* (god), *-natha* (lord), *-dasa* (servant), *-priya* (beloved)
- Many names encode a deity's epithet (*Krishna*-, *Rama*-, *Shiva*-, *Vishnu*-, *Lakshmi*-)
- **Nakshatra-based naming**: Traditional naming uses the child's birth *nakshatra* (lunar constellation, 27 in number) to select an auspicious first syllable.

### Lineage
The *gotra* system — patrilineal lineages traced to sage-ancestors (*rishis*). Gotras govern marriage (exogamy) and ritual (which mantras, which fires). Every Brahmin and many other-caste Hindus know their gotra.

### Journey/Origin
The Aryan / Indo-Aryan migration narrative (whether accepted as historical migration or rejected in Out-of-India theories). The *Saraswati* tradition ties identity to a specific sacred landscape.

### Season
Hindu calendar is lunisolar with extensive festival cycle. The *panchang* encodes tithi (lunar day), vara (weekday), nakshatra, yoga, karana — any of which can be analysed against a birth date.

### Diaspora
Significant in Southeast Asia historically (Cambodia, Thailand, Indonesia, Bali); modern diaspora across Fiji, Trinidad, Guyana, Mauritius, South Africa, UK, US, Canada. Gotra and nakshatra-based naming often preserved.

### Landscape
The seven sacred cities; the seven sacred rivers; Varanasi (Kashi); the pilgrimage circuits.

### Voice
The *brahmin* (Vedic reciter); the *kathakar* (story-teller); the *pandit*.
