## Pattern Axes

### Names
The Arabic/Islamic name system has five formal components:
- **Ism** — personal name (Muhammad, Ali, Fatima, etc.)
- **Kunya** — teknonym (Abu [son]; Umm [daughter])
- **Nasab** — patronymic chain (*ibn [father] ibn [grandfather]...*)
- **Laqab** — epithet (al-Rashid "the rightly-guided"; al-Kabir "the great")
- **Nisba** — adjectival affiliation (tribal, geographic, or professional: al-Baghdadi, al-Qurashi, al-Misri)

**Engine relevance:** the *nisba* often encodes ancestral geography or tribal affiliation across centuries of diaspora.

### Lineage
*Nasab* chains can be recited back for dozens of generations. This is one of the most elaborate genealogical traditions in world history.

### Diaspora
Historical diaspora across the entire Islamic world from Spain to Indonesia; modern diaspora in Europe and the Americas. Naming conventions remain highly stable across diaspora contexts.

### Voice
The *hafiz* (Qur'an memoriser); the *rawi* (transmitter); the poet (*sha'ir*). Oral transmission is the core mechanism.
