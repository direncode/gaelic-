## Pattern Axes

### Names
Greek names are extensively meaningful. Common elements:
- *Theo-/Zeu-/Pan-/Apollo-* (theophoric)
- *-krat-* ("rule, power"); *-menes* ("mind, strength"); *-philos* ("loved, beloved")
- Patronymics in *-ides, -iades* (Atreides = "son of Atreus")

### Lineage
The *genos* (clan); deme affiliation in Athens; tribal identity (Ionian, Dorian, Aeolian, Achaean) tied to migration traditions.

### Origin/Journey
Extensive migration narratives: Dorian migration, Ionian colonisation, Achaean returns from Troy, the founding of colonies (Magna Graecia, the Black Sea, North Africa).

### Claim
Foundation narratives (*ktisis*) for every major city name the founder and the oracular consultation. Many cities claim descent from a specific hero-ancestor.

### Season
Athenian calendar; Olympiad counting; festival cycle (Dionysia, Panathenaia, Olympia).

### Landscape
The specific sacred sites: Delphi, Olympia, Eleusis, Delos. Every region of Greece carries mythological weight.

### Diaspora
Colonial diaspora (Magna Graecia, the Black Sea); modern diaspora across the Americas, Australia, Europe. Naming practices remain remarkably stable; patron saint names add a Christian layer without replacing the ancient forms.

### Voice
The *rhapsode* (epic reciter); the *poietes* (poet); the chorus in tragedy.
