## Pattern Axes

### Names
Chinese names are kanji-encoded with specific meaning. Traditional structure:
- **Xing** (姓) — surname (historically one character, some two-character surnames)
- **Ming** (名) — personal name (often two characters)
- **Zi** (字) — courtesy name (historical)
- **Hao** (號) — art/literary name (historical)

**Generation names (*zi bei*, 字輩)**: Many clans maintain a generation-poem whose characters are used in successive generations' given names. A child's name encodes their exact generational position.

### Lineage
Extensive *zupu* (clan genealogy) tradition; patrilineal emphasis. The Confucian filial piety framework makes ancestor-reverence central.

### Season
Lunar calendar; *bazi* (eight characters) birth-chart system encodes year, month, day, hour in stems-and-branches.

### Diaspora
Chinese diaspora across Southeast Asia, the Americas, Europe — generation names and surnames remain stable; clan associations (*huiguan*) preserve lineage memory.

### Voice
The scholar-official; the clan elder; the ancestor-tablet (*pái wèi*).
