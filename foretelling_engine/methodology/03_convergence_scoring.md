# METHODOLOGY 03: CONVERGENCE SCORING

## The Honest Rubric That Separates Findings From Horoscopes

---

This is the rubric. It is what keeps the Foretelling Engine honest. Every biographical element must be scored against every relevant textual pattern using this scale, and the scores must be justified.

Without this rubric, the engine degenerates into pattern-stretching. With it, strong findings land with the weight they deserve, and weak findings are honestly flagged so they cannot discredit the whole.

---

## THE FOUR TIERS

### ★★★★ STRONG

The biographical fact and the tradition's core claim coincide **directly and verifiably**, with:

- A named, published source in the corpus
- A named, attested linguistic or textual feature
- No interpretive stretching required
- A skeptical reader checking the source will agree

**Examples of STRONG convergences:**

- Mudiwa's surname *Mtemererwa* contains the verb *tema* (Proto-Bantu *\*-téma*), which is the verb that built Great Zimbabwe (Wikipedia, Hodza & Fortune, multiple scholarly sources). **Direct textual claim + direct linguistic fact = STRONG.**
- Arran's given name is the cognate of *Árann*, the Irish name for the Aran Islands off Galway — his own ancestral coastline. **Direct linguistic fact + geographic specificity = STRONG.**
- The Shona theological principle that *vadzimu* travel with descendants is stated directly in the tradition: *"They guard their people wherever they may be… in America, England and India, but the vadzimu still see these people."* Applied to a Birmingham-born child, this is a **direct doctrinal fit = STRONG.**

### ★★★ MODERATE

The parallel is structurally sound but requires **interpretive framing**. The source exists, the claim is real, but connecting it to the biography requires one or more interpretive moves that a sceptical reader could reasonably question.

**Examples of MODERATE convergences:**

- Arran's birthplace (Riyadh) falls geographically between the *Lebor Gabála*'s stated Egyptian and Scythian origin points. **The text is real, the geography is real, but the interpretive leap ("Arabia counts as the same eastern world") requires framing.** MODERATE.
- The name *Mudiwa Mtemererwa* functions as a compressed *nhetembo*. **The praise tradition is real, the morphology is real, but calling the name itself a "praise poem" involves an interpretive extension of the scholarly definition.** MODERATE.
- The multi-bearer Mtemererwa family names (George, Innocence, Mudiwa) read together as a coherent lineage declaration. **The names are real, the pattern is visible, but whether the family consciously coordinated this versus it emerging organically is not certain.** MODERATE.

### ★★ WEAK

The parallel is **present but thin**. Either:

- The biographical fact is only adjacent to the tradition's claim, not coincident with it
- The tradition's claim is general rather than specific
- The evidence is circumstantial

WEAK convergences **must be included honestly** — hiding them is not an option — but their thinness must be acknowledged openly. They do not carry evidential weight.

**Examples of WEAK convergences:**

- Mudiwa's birthdate of 18 May falls in the same month as Africa Day (25 May) and shares the day-number (18) with Zimbabwe Independence Day (April 18). **The calendrical association is real but proximate, not coincident.** WEAK.
- Arran's birth on November 21 falls in the broader post-Samhain period. **The Samhain liminality is real, but Samhain itself is November 1, and the texts do not single out November 21.** WEAK.

### ★ NONE

**No parallel exists.** The engine does not manufacture one.

This is an active output, not a silent omission. When a biographical element is checked against the tradition's claims and nothing aligns, the output is explicitly: *"No significant parallel."* The engine records this, because finding nothing is an honest finding.

**Example:**

- If Mudiwa's exact birth hour had been known and checked against Shona lunar calendar practice, and no correspondence had been found, the rubric would record: *"Birth hour: NONE."* Not "maybe it corresponds to something we haven't found yet." Just: *no parallel.*

## THE SCORING PROCEDURE

For each biographical element — name, date, place, heritage, landscape, season, lineage, etc. — perform the following:

1. **List every tradition axis** the element could plausibly match (origin, journey, name-as-claim, diaspora, landscape, etc.)
2. **For each axis**, ask:
   - Is the tradition's claim on this axis **specific enough** that a biographical coincidence would be meaningful?
   - Does the biographical fact **directly coincide**, or only approach?
   - What **source** would verify the match?
3. **Assign the tier:** STRONG / MODERATE / WEAK / NONE
4. **Write a one-sentence justification** stating the evidence and the interpretive move (if any)
5. **Cite the source**

| # | Element | Axis | Strength | Evidence | Source |

## THE PUBLICATION THRESHOLD

A Foretelling Engine analysis is considered to have produced a **publishable finding** when the convergence table meets the following threshold:

> **At minimum: 3 STRONG convergences, OR 4 convergences of MODERATE-or-better.**

If the threshold is not met, the analysis concludes with:

> *"Convergence threshold not met. No significant Foretelling finding. The biography does not, at the level of the corpus queried, structurally recur the tradition's pattern."*

This is a valid output. It is also a **crucial** output. The engine's credibility rests on its willingness to report null findings.

---

## WEIGHTED SUMMARY

When a finding meets the threshold and is published, the narrative opens with a **weight assessment**:

- **Total STRONG:** [N]
- **Total MODERATE:** [N]
- **Total WEAK (flagged honestly):** [N]
- **Overall weight:** [MAJOR / SUBSTANTIAL / MODEST / MARGINAL]

**Weight classifications:**

- **MAJOR** — 6+ STRONG convergences with minimal weak points (the Mtemererwa lineage finding reaches this)
- **SUBSTANTIAL** — 4–5 STRONG convergences, supporting moderates (both Arran and Mudiwa singly reach this)
- **MODEST** — 3 STRONG or equivalent; real finding but limited scope
- **MARGINAL** — at threshold; honest inclusion that the finding is narrow

No finding is ever "EXTREME" in the scoring register. The rubric does not permit superlatives. Superlatives belong to the narrative register, and only if earned by the scholarly one.

## FAILURE MODES TO AVOID

### FAILURE 1: Rating Up To Fit a Desired Conclusion

**Bad:** "I want this to be a big finding, so I'll rate this MODERATE instead of WEAK."
**Why bad:** Corrupts the rubric. The reader cannot trust any rating once one is inflated. Every rating bears the weight of all the others.

### FAILURE 2: Hiding WEAK Findings

**Bad:** "This one doesn't really fit, so I'll just leave it out."
**Why bad:** Sceptical readers will find it themselves and dismiss the whole finding. Include it, rate it WEAK, keep it visible.

### FAILURE 3: Claiming STRONG Without a Source

**Bad:** "This feels like a strong match."
**Why bad:** STRONG requires a named source. If you can't cite the source, the rating is at most MODERATE pending documentation.

### FAILURE 4: Conflating Tiers

**Bad:** "I have 9 convergences" (without tier breakdown).
**Why bad:** Nine all-WEAK convergences do not equal one STRONG. Always report the tier breakdown.

### FAILURE 5: Manufacturing a Convergence Where None Exists

**Bad:** Seeing that the birth date falls on, say, the 14th, and searching desperately for any tradition item involving the number 14.
**Why bad:** This is the single most tempting failure mode. Rule: if the first pass surfaces nothing, mark the axis NONE and move on.

---

## THE DISCIPLINE IN ONE SENTENCE

> **Rate honestly. Cite rigorously. Publish the null results. Let the strong findings carry their own weight.**

If an analyst rates every convergence STRONG, the engine is compromised. If an analyst is willing to mark most convergences as NONE when nothing aligns, and only the real ones as STRONG, the engine's rare big findings will land with genuine force.

The Mtemererwa analysis reached MAJOR weight (6 STRONG with multi-bearer lineage) precisely because the scoring was disciplined elsewhere — the May 18 date was flagged WEAK, the specific family surname etymology was initially flagged as unverified. Those honest markings are what let the strong findings ring true.

*The rubric is the referee. Without it, the analyst is both player and judge — and the finding becomes unverifiable.*
